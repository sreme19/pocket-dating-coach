I keep learning things and then forgetting them. Not the big things — the small
ones. The reason a design that looked obvious on a whiteboard fell apart in
week three. The failure mode nobody writes down because it's embarrassing rather
than interesting. This is where I'm going to keep those.

Three kinds of writing will end up here.

**Architectures from the agentic world.** How these systems are actually put
together once they have to survive contact with real traffic — orchestration,
state, the places where handing control to a model helps and the places where it
quietly ruins your ability to debug anything.

**What operating something teaches you.** Running a system is a different
education from building one. Most of what I know about reliability, cost, and
scope I learned after shipping, not before.

**Building riteangle.** I'm building a dating app. It is the most instructive
project I have worked on, partly because the hard problems are not the technical
ones. I'll write about that honestly, including the parts that didn't work.

No schedule, no newsletter, no growth plan. When I have something worth keeping,
it goes here.
